---

## 🛠️ How to Install Chrome Extension (3 Easy Steps)

1. **Download & Unzip** `browser-homepage-extension.zip` on your computer.
2. Open Google Chrome (or Brave / Microsoft Edge) and navigate to **`chrome://extensions`**.
3. Turn on the **Developer mode** toggle in the top right corner, click **Load unpacked**, and select the unzipped folder.

*Open a new tab and enjoy your new homepage!*

## ✨ Key Features in v1.0.0

- **New Tab Override & Toolbar Popup:** Seamlessly replaces your default new tab page and gives you instant access to quick links from the extension toolbar popup.
- **Native Secure Gmail Integration:** Authenticates natively via Chrome Identity (`chrome.identity`) API. Tokens are stored securely inside your browser without any third-party backend.
- **Developer Widgets:**
  - **GitHub:** Live stats, repositories, follower counts, and contribution streak visualization.
  - **LeetCode:** Real-time problem solved counters, contest ratings, and difficulty breakdowns.
- **Custom Bento Flip Cards:** Add customized shortcuts with custom titles, URLs, and optional custom icon URLs.
- **Interactive Design:** Rich sleek dark aesthetic with interactive glassmorphism and custom scrollbar styling.
